Well, Master Colins, all is past and over; you have got your place again, and all is well. Coot now, let me admonish you for the future to be quiet and hear reason; moderate your choler and your passions and your partialities: it is not for a clown like you to prattle and dispute with me; in fait you should know better. Mort. Come, come, it is you that should know better; in this poor Higlander the force of prejudice has some plea, because he is a clown; but you, a citizen that should be of the world, whose heart philosophy and travel might have opened, should know better than to join the cry with those, whose charity, like the limitation of a brief, stops short at Berwick, and never circulates beyond the Tweed. By Heaven, I Would rather weed out one such unmanly prejudice from the hearts of my countrymen, than add another Indies to their empire. END OF THE PLAY.
