She seems their direst rage to brave, Till rising from the yawning wave, DESPAIR APPEARS, THE SPIRIT OF THE DEEP! The whelming surge, her flaming pinions sweep; The howling winds, with louder clamours roar; The angry billows lash the rocky shore; While livid lightnings, flashing round, Quench their blue arrows in the gulf profound! And now, the flinty fabric shakes! And now, DESPONDENCY awakes! And, rising from her hanging seat, Darts forth, DESPAIR to meet; And, locked within her cold embrace, Sinks, 'midst the horrors OF TH' INFURIATE SPACE! Now, the dreary tempest over, Maddening HORROR reigns no more! On the EASTERN summit bright DAY unbars the gates of Light! And rushing forward, robed in crimson fire, Bids sombre NIGHT, and all HER TRAIN, RETIRE! The severing clouds dissolving fly; The soft breeze fans the glittering main! The lucid rill runs babbling over the plain, Its crystal breast refleas the glowing sky! HOPE comes, in heavenly colours dressed; Her golden pinions fan my breast; Her eye with spreading lustre shines; Her hand a beauteous chaplet twines; And pointing FAME's fair temple 'midst the skies, Bids, for my GRATEFUL BROW, a spreading LAUREL rise!
